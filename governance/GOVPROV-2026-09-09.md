# Context

The project identified that artifact authorship and production-tool metadata
were not formally required. This gap meant:

- agents and humans could create artifacts without declaring their role,
- tool versions could not be tracked,
- reproducibility and credibility could not be established at the production
  level.

This session formalizes the requirement across governance and documentation.

# Rationale

Making authorship mandatory at the artifact level:

- closes a traceability gap identified in RG007 (governed work must record
  what governed it; that includes who produced it and using what tools),
- enables future audits and quality reviews to distinguish human-created from
  agent-created artifacts,
- documents agent versions, which may affect reproducibility or credibility,
- is consistent with Project Zero's commitment to disciplined, traceable
  research.

The new rule RG008 is versioned independently so it can be cited and evolved
without rewriting existing governance artifacts.
