# Statement

No accepted governance rule MAY remain only in conversation.

Every accepted rule MUST be persisted in the repository before further design continues.

---

# Rationale

The repository is the single Source of Truth.

Knowledge that exists only in conversation is effectively lost.

---

# Consequences

Following this rule:

- preserves institutional knowledge,
- prevents forgotten agreements,
- keeps documentation synchronized with reasoning.

Ignoring this rule creates undocumented governance debt.

---

# Applies To

All governance rules.

---

# Exceptions

Proposed rules awaiting discussion MAY remain in conversation until accepted.
