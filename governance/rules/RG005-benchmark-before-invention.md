# Statement

Established practices SHOULD be studied before introducing new methodologies.

Novel approaches SHOULD explicitly state why existing practices are insufficient.

---

# Rationale

Project Zero values evidence over originality.

Understanding existing approaches reduces duplicated effort and exposes blind spots.

---

# Consequences

Following this rule:

- encourages informed innovation,
- reduces unnecessary complexity,
- improves confidence in methodological decisions.

Ignoring this rule increases the risk of reinventing existing solutions.

---

# Applies To

Research methodology.

Governance evolution.

Major architectural decisions.

---

# Exceptions

When exploring genuinely novel problems with no established body of knowledge.
