# Research

This directory contains the research foundation of Project Zero.

Its purpose is not to document software.

Its purpose is to document the evolution of knowledge.

---

# Philosophy

Research is treated as a continuous learning process.

Knowledge is built through disciplined experimentation rather than intuition or isolated success.

Every document in this directory should contribute to answering the project's central research question.

---

# Reading Order

New contributors (human or AI) should begin with:

1. 000-north-star.md
2. 001-problem-definition.md
3. 002-edge.md
4. 003-research-methodology.md

These documents define:

- why the project exists,
- what problem it addresses,
- how trading edge is currently understood,
- how research is conducted.

---

# Relationship to Other Directories

This directory does not contain every research artifact.

The repository organizes knowledge by maturity.

research/

Foundational concepts and research documents.

↓

hypotheses/

Testable claims.

↓

experiments/

Executed investigations.

↓

discoveries/

Evidence-backed findings.

↓

decisions/

Operational and architectural decisions.

↓

governance/

Long-term project rules.

Knowledge flows through these stages as confidence increases.

Research sessions are recorded under research/sessions/.

As of 2026-09-04 the experiments/ and discoveries/ stages exist but are
intentionally empty: no experiment has been executed yet. See their READMEs
for the requirements an artifact must meet before entering those stages.

---

# Guiding Principles

Research should:

- begin with important questions,
- encourage competing hypotheses,
- value negative results,
- preserve reasoning,
- remain open to revision.

No document in this directory should be considered permanent truth.

---

# Scope

This directory intentionally focuses on understanding the problem before proposing technical solutions.

Software, automation, and AI are considered potential outcomes of research—not the purpose of research.
