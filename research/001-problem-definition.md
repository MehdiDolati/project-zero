# Problem Definition

## Context

Financial markets provide opportunities for generating returns, but consistently identifying and maintaining profitable trading approaches is an extremely difficult problem.

A solo researcher has access to many resources:
- books and academic research,
- trading platforms,
- market data,
- strategy development tools,
- machine learning and AI technologies.

However, access to resources does not automatically translate into a sustainable trading capability.

The challenge is not simply finding a profitable strategy once. The challenge is building a repeatable process that can continuously discover, validate, manage, and retire trading ideas based on evidence.

---

# Core Problem

How can a solo researcher systematically discover, validate, deploy, monitor, and retire trading edge to achieve sustainable financial freedom?

---

# Problem Decomposition

The problem consists of several interconnected challenges.

## 1. Edge Discovery

Current priority: High

Approximate weight: 50%

The primary challenge is discovering trading approaches that contain a genuine and persistent advantage.

This includes:

- generating trading hypotheses,
- researching market behaviors,
- designing strategies,
- avoiding overfitting,
- validating whether observed performance represents real edge or randomness.

---

## 2. Trading System Management

Current priority: Medium

Approximate weight: 25%

Finding an edge is not sufficient.

A sustainable system requires mechanisms for:

- portfolio construction,
- capital allocation,
- risk management,
- strategy weighting,
- strategy monitoring,
- strategy retirement,
- portfolio-level decision making.

---

## 3. Decision Psychology

Current priority: Medium

Approximate weight: 25%

A trading system is ultimately executed by humans.

The process must account for:

- emotional decisions,
- inconsistent execution,
- changing rules during drawdowns,
- overconfidence after success,
- inability to abandon failing ideas.

---

# Desired Outcome

The desired outcome is not certainty.

Financial markets are inherently uncertain.

The goal is to develop a disciplined learning and decision-making process that improves the probability of making profitable decisions over time.

---

# Current Hypothesis

Sustainable trading success is not primarily achieved by finding isolated profitable strategies.

It emerges from a complete system capable of:

- generating hypotheses,
- collecting evidence,
- validating assumptions,
- allocating resources,
- learning from outcomes,
- continuously improving.

---

# Non-Goals

At this stage, this project is not:

- a strategy generator,
- a trading robot,
- a backtesting engine,
- an AI trading agent,
- a commercial trading platform.

Those may become future automation opportunities, but they are not the current problem.

---

# Research Question

How can an independent researcher build a repeatable, evidence-based system for discovering and managing sustainable trading edge?
