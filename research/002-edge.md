# Edge Definition

## Working Definition

Trading edge is not considered a property of an isolated strategy.

It is an emergent property created by the interaction between:

- market behavior,
- research methodology,
- strategy design,
- validation process,
- risk management,
- execution capability,
- continuous adaptation.

---

# What Edge Is Not

Edge is not:

- a single profitable backtest,
- a high historical return,
- a specific indicator,
- a fixed strategy,
- a guaranteed future outcome.

---

# Components of Edge

## Strategy Edge

Ability to identify and exploit repeatable market behaviors.

## Research Edge

Ability to discover, test, and reject ideas better than others.

## Data Edge

Access to higher quality or more relevant information.

## Execution Edge

Ability to convert theoretical performance into real-world results.

## Risk Edge

Ability to survive uncertainty and allocate capital efficiently.

## Adaptation Edge

Ability to detect changing conditions and evolve.

---

# Edge Lifecycle

Edge should be treated as a lifecycle:

Discovery → Validation → Deployment → Monitoring → Adaptation → Retirement
