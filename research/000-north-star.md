# North Star

## Purpose

The purpose of this project is to develop the capability to achieve sustainable financial freedom through disciplined, evidence-based discovery and management of opportunities in uncertain environments.

---

# The Ultimate Goal

The ultimate goal is not building software.

The ultimate goal is not creating a trading strategy.

The ultimate goal is not achieving a specific financial target.

The ultimate goal is:

**Creating the freedom to live according to personal values by building a sustainable and continuously improving decision-making capability.**

---

# Why This Matters

Financial independence provides the ability to choose how time and attention are spent.

It enables:

- continuous learning,
- research and exploration,
- creative work,
- physical and mental well-being,
- meaningful experiences,
- freedom from environments that conflict with personal values.

Money is not the final objective.

Money is the mechanism that enables autonomy.

---

# Core Belief

In complex and uncertain domains, lasting success does not come from isolated decisions or individual successes.

It comes from building systems that can:

- learn continuously,
- adapt to changing conditions,
- distinguish evidence from assumptions,
- preserve knowledge,
- improve decisions over time.

---

# Project Philosophy

Think Big. Build Small.

The vision should be ambitious.

The execution should be disciplined.

Large outcomes are achieved through small, validated steps.

---

# Principles

## 1. Truth Over Attachment

No idea, strategy, tool, architecture, or project name is sacred.

Ideas must survive criticism and evidence.

The objective is not proving ourselves right.

The objective is getting closer to reality.

---

## 2. Method Before Automation

Software and AI are tools.

They should only be introduced when a valuable, repeatable process has already been identified.

Automation amplifies a good process.

It also amplifies a bad process.

---

## 3. Evidence Over Opinion

Beliefs should be continuously updated based on evidence.

Confidence is earned through validation, not enthusiasm.

---

## 4. Learning Over Winning

Failures are valuable research outcomes.

A rejected hypothesis with clear reasoning creates knowledge.

---

# Current Direction

The current research direction is exploring whether a solo researcher can build a systematic process to:

- discover potential trading edge,
- validate it rigorously,
- manage multiple opportunities,
- learn from real-world outcomes,
- continuously improve the decision-making process.

---

# What Success Means

Success is not defined by certainty.

Certainty is not achievable in complex adaptive systems.

Success means:

- making better decisions over time,
- reducing avoidable mistakes,
- improving the quality of learning,
- increasing the probability of favorable outcomes.

---

# Open Questions

This document intentionally does not answer:

- What is a true trading edge?
- Which markets should be studied?
- Which tools should be used?
- Should software be built?
- What automation is valuable?

Those questions belong to future research.

---

# Final Statement

This project exists to discover whether a disciplined, evidence-based system can transform uncertainty into better decisions and create a path toward sustainable freedom.
