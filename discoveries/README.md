# Discoveries

Discoveries are evidence-backed findings that have survived evaluation.

They sit at the high-confidence end of the knowledge maturity ladder defined
in [research-003](../research/003-research-methodology.md):

Observation → Evidence → Claim → Knowledge

A Discovery is a claim supported by reproducible experimental evidence that is
sufficiently trusted to influence future decisions — always remaining open to
revision.

---

# Requirements

A finding belongs here only when it:

- originates from one or more recorded experiments
  (see [experiments/](../experiments/README.md)),
- references its supporting evidence and decisions
  ([G005-traceability](../governance/G005-traceability.md)),
- states its confidence and remaining uncertainty,
- remains open to challenge.

Negative results and rejected hypotheses are discoveries too: they reduce
uncertainty and prevent repeated mistakes.
